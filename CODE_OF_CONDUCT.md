# Code of Conduct

`pysteer` aims to be a respectful, practical, and research-friendly space.

By participating, contributors agree to:

- Treat other people with respect and patience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical problems.
- Avoid harassment, exclusionary language, and personal attacks.
- Help keep discussions useful for maintainers and future contributors.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when behavior undermines a constructive project environment.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`.
